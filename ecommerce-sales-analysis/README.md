# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Tableau to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, customer purchasing behavior, and country-level sales performance.

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- CSV Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Key Insights
- The United Kingdom generated the highest revenue by a large margin
- Revenue peaked significantly during November, suggesting strong seasonal demand
- A small number of products generated a large share of total revenue
- High-value customers contributed disproportionately to total sales

## Dashboard Visualizations
- Revenue by Country
- Top Products
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Top Customers

## Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing regions
- Prepare inventory for seasonal demand spikes
- Retain high-value customers with loyalty strategies
- Promote top-selling products more aggressively
